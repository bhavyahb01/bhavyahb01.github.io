Pilot plant · NRCan

From separate unit tests to a continuous extraction process.

Commissioned and optimized a pilot-scale solvent extraction system by testing the variables that actually controlled performance: pump rates, mixing, flows and weir heights.

  • 5 operating & sampling SOPs

  • Procurement and vendor coordination

02

MSc research · University of Alberta

A greener route to lithium from Alberta oilfield brines.

Designed biopolymer binders for manganese-based adsorbents in direct lithium extraction, moving between batch experiments and early column testing to study uptake, selectivity, regeneration and material stability.

  • 15 formulations evaluated
  • 5 regeneration cycles
  • 2 posters + 1 oral presentation
150+experiments conducted
03

AACE Class 4 · Imperial Oil capstone

A refinery concept that beat the CO specification by 86%.

Led a five-person team through alternative screening, simulation, equipment definition, process safety and economics for a hydrogen recycle stream.

  • 9 kg/h CO removed
  • $37M projected NPV
  • PFD, P&ID, HAZOP & PHA
14ppmv CO vs. 100 target
04

Academic process design · Lead removal

A fixed-bed concept for battery-industry wastewater.

Developed a conceptual adsorption process to treat 864 L/day of lead-acid battery effluent. The work covered technology and adsorbent screening, Langmuir modelling, column sizing, residence time, regeneration and pressure-drop evaluation.

  • 30-day operating cycle
  • Three-column configuration
  • AMO-MOF selected from four finalists
15→<0.01mg/L Pb(II) design target
